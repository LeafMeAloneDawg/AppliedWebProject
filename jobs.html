Community Support Worker

Reference: CSW021

Salary: $68,000 – $74,000 per annum, pro rata

Reports to: Community Programs Manager

Provide direct support to individuals and families accessing our community programs, helping them connect with local resources and build long-term wellbeing.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Conduct initial client intake and needs assessments
  2. Coordinate referrals to relevant support services
  3. Maintain accurate case notes and client records
  4. Attend weekly team case review meetings

Essential Requirements

  • Diploma in Community Services or related field
  • Current Working with Children Check
  • Minimum 1 year experience in a support role

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience working with culturally diverse communities
  • First Aid certification

Volunteer Program Coordinator

Reference: VPC114

Salary: $72,000 – $80,000 per annum

Reports to: Operations Manager

Recruit, train, and support our volunteer network, ensuring volunteers are well matched to programs and feel valued in their contribution.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Recruit and onboard new volunteers
  2. Organise volunteer training sessions
  3. Schedule volunteer rosters for community events
  4. Recognise volunteer contributions through regular appreciation events

Essential Requirements

  • Experience coordinating teams or volunteer groups
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Current driver's licence

Preferable Requirements

  • Experience with volunteer management software
  • Background in event planning

Youth Outreach Officer

Reference: YOO307

Salary: $65,000 – $70,000 per annum, pro rata

Reports to: Youth Services Team Leader

Engage with young people in the community through outreach programs, mentoring, and after-school activities that build confidence and connection.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Facilitate after-school and holiday youth programs
  2. Build trusted relationships with young people and their families
  3. Identify and respond to early warning signs of disengagement
  4. Liaise with local schools and youth services

Essential Requirements

  • Certificate IV in Youth Work or related field
  • Current Working with Children Check
  • Availability for after-school and weekend work

Preferable Requirements

  • Lived experience or strong connection to the local community
  • Experience running group activities or workshops
